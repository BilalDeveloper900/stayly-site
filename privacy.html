Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

Stayly counts the days you spend in each country. Everything it knows stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We have no server, no account system and no analytics, so we never receive your data.

What Stayly stores

  • Your home country.
  • Your trips: country, arrival date and leaving date.
  • Your counters and settings, like "Schengen 90/180" or "warn me 10 days before".

All of this is saved on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, your trips and counters are also kept in your private iCloud, so they come back if you reinstall Stayly or get a new iPhone. Only you can see them, not us. Apple handles iCloud under its own privacy policy. Your home country and settings stay on the iPhone.

Location

Stayly uses your location only if you turn on Automatic tracking (a Premium feature). Then:

  • Stayly uses the iPhone's low-power location, which updates only when you move far.
  • The country is found on your iPhone using a map of country borders built into the app. Your location is never sent anywhere, not to us and not to anyone else.
  • Only the country and the date are saved. Your exact location is not kept.

You can turn Automatic tracking off in Stayly's Settings, or stop location access in the iPhone Settings app at any time.

Notifications

Warnings about your day limits are made on your iPhone and shown as local notifications. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Stayly Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details.

Exports

When you export a PDF or CSV, the file is made on your iPhone. It goes only where you choose to share it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or logins.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of data. We have none to share.

Deleting your data

Open Stayly, go to Settings and tap Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes everything from your iPhone.

Children

Stayly is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
